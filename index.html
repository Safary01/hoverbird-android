How to Play

Simple to start.
Hard to master.

Everything you need to fly, score, and claim your spot on the leaderboard.

1

Create Your Account

Register with your username, email, and phone number. Your username is what the world sees on the leaderboard — choose wisely.

2

Tap to Flap

Your bird falls with gravity. Tap the screen (or press Space) to flap upward. Too fast and you hit the ceiling. Too slow and you crash. Find your rhythm.

3

Dodge the Pipes

Green pipes come from the right. Fly through the gap. Touching any pipe or the ground ends the game immediately. Each pair you pass scores +1 point.

4

Two Scores — One Goal

Your Best Score is saved forever. Your Season Score resets each month. Only your highest game score counts — play often, play better.

5

Compete Every Month

The top 3 players each season enter the Hall of Fame forever and qualify for a monthly reward. Then it all resets — fresh season, fresh chance.

Privacy Policy

Your data.
Your rights.

Last updated: June 2026 · Nigeria

🇳🇬 This policy complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 issued by NITDA.

📦 What We Collect

  • Account data: Username, email address, and phone number.
  • Game data: Your scores (best score, season score, games played) and leaderboard position.
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Reward data (top 3 only): Bank details collected privately only when you qualify for a monthly reward. Deleted after payment.

🎯 Why We Collect It

  • To create and manage your player account.
  • To save your scores and display your rank on the leaderboard.
  • To record monthly Hall of Fame champions by username and score.
  • To send monthly rewards to qualifying top-3 players.
  • To detect and prevent cheating or unauthorised access.

👁️ Who Can See Your Data

Your username and season score are visible to other players on the leaderboard. Your email and phone number are never shown to other players. The HoverBird admin can view all data for game management purposes. Google Firebase processes data per their own privacy agreement. We never sell your data.

⚖️ Your Rights (NDPR)

  • Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: Ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion within 30 days.
  • Objection: Object to how we process your data.

You may also contact NITDA at nitda.gov.ng if you believe your rights have been violated.

👦 Children

HoverBird is for players aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has registered, contact us immediately.

🍪 Cookies & Storage

Firebase uses browser storage to maintain your login session. These are essential for the game to work. We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.
